# Story-blocking traversal audit

## Question

Which story, badge, key-item, forced-battle, transport, and field-move checks stop the player from traveling through the regions shipped by HNS, FRLG, and Emerald? Which of those checks can be made nonblocking with a small, localized change that leaves the related story available?

## Scope and method

This audit covers:

- HNS: Johto, Kanto, Alola, and Sinjoh.
- FireRed and LeafGreen: Kanto and the Sevii Islands. Both products use the same FRLG map set for the gates below.
- Emerald: Hoenn.

| Product | Region | Listed blockers |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| HNS | Johto | 18 |
| HNS | Kanto | 7 |
| HNS | Alola | 2 |
| HNS | Sinjoh | 4 |
| FireRed and LeafGreen | Kanto | 12 |
| FireRed and LeafGreen | Sevii Islands | 6 |
| Emerald | Hoenn | 25 |

A blocker is included when it closes a settlement exit, map connection, through-route, regional branch, transport link, or the only normal entrance to a region. A Gym, dungeon, or story encounter is included only when it supplies state or a field move needed by one of those connections. Optional dead-end interiors, item rooms, and convenience-only fast travel are excluded unless noted.

The audit traced each product's `map.json` objects and coordinate events through its `scripts.inc` state changes, then checked shared field-move authorization and transport code. Collision-only checks were used for a few object and terrain candidates. Those checks do not model every ledge, elevation, or scripted map mutation, so the affected rows call for a short in-game traversal test.

In evidence cells, bare map paths such as `Route101/scripts.inc` are relative to `game/data/maps/`. Paths beginning with `game/` are relative to the repository root.

## What counts as a trivial change

A suggestion appears only when the bypass can stay local to one map or transport script: remove or narrow a turnback trigger, move one blocker off a choke point, stop installing a blocking metatile, or relax a self-contained item or state check. A dash means no complete, low-risk trivial change was found.

These are research candidates, not implementation approval. Every suggested lane or object move still needs a collision check and an emulator walkthrough. Suggestions must not synthesize unrelated story-completion flags merely to open a road.

## Cross-product implementation notes

- Do not change shared field-move functions globally to solve one product's route. Emerald, FRLG, and HNS use different badge mappings, and several gates still require HM ownership and a compatible party member after a badge check is removed.
- Prefer opening a lane over setting a broad story-completion flag. Hide flags dedicated to roadblock objects are safer than flags that also authorize rewards, encounters, transport initialization, or postgame state.
- Keep first-arrival initialization on HNS Kanto and Sinjoh paths. An early warp that skips the existing S.S. Aqua disembarkation or Meara setup can leave regional state uninitialized.
- Collision candidates in Cliff Edge Gate, HNS Victory Road, Snowswept Cavern, and lane-based trigger suggestions need a short emulator walkthrough before implementation.

## Source limitations

This is a static source audit, not a completed playthrough of every branch. Scripted gates, flags, object coordinates, warps, and field-move checks are exact at the audited revision. A collision graph supported selected topology claims, but it did not simulate directional ledges, every elevation rule, or all runtime metatile changes. The uncertain cases are labeled rather than promoted to confirmed blockers.
